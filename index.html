A SMALL GUIDE FOR A LARGE CASTLE

Let curiosity lead.

  1. Explore the rooms. Click a doorway or use the arrow keys / WASD. The view always faces north; south is behind you.
  2. Answer to unlock a door. You get two attempts. A second miss reveals an explanation, then offers a fresh question. Open doors stay open.
  3. Find the stairs. The gold stair symbol is always visible on your map. Torches reveal a route for 12 seconds. You have five for the whole journey.
  4. Restore the castle. Reach your point goal, then use the stairs on your current floor. Before reaching the goal, stairs lead to a new floor.

Take the scenic route

Click the inhabitant to talk, or the framed atlas to read. Wikipedia research is encouraged: looking up a question removes its speed bonus, but the door and base points are still yours to earn. Study mode has no speed bonus or countdown.

Base points: 150 / 250 / 400 / 600 by question tier. Classic adds up to 200 speed points, decreasing by four each second. A first wrong attempt reduces the possible award by 50. Your existing score is never reduced.

1–4 answer · T torch · J journal · L library · Esc close a panel. Menus pause the active timer. Sound is optional and starts muted.

About this little castle

reMindMaze is an independent tribute to Encarta MindMaze, with original code, procedural artwork, characters, and dialogue. It contains no Encarta assets and is not affiliated with Microsoft or the Wikimedia Foundation.

The starter pack contains 108 original questions informed by 27 Wikipedia articles. Source links and explanations accompany every question. The pack is curated; live Wikipedia pages are for reading, not automatically generated questions. Questions repeat after the selected pool is exhausted.
